# Building a Small Garden

A small garden begins with one clear purpose: growing a few useful plants well.

## Choose the Site

Observe the site for seven days. Record the hours of direct sunlight, the direction of the wind, and where rainwater collects.

![A simple garden plan](images/garden-plan.png)

## Prepare the Soil

Add mature compost, then loosen the top 20 centimeters of soil. Avoid working the ground when it is saturated with water.

## Review the Result

After four weeks, compare plant health with the notes from your first survey. Change one variable at a time so that the result remains understandable.
